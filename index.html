How it plays

Draw the line. Take the ground.

Arcade rules, tuned for a modern screen. Every cut is a gamble against something that wants it broken.

  1. Run the border

    Your marker runs the outer edge. Steer into open ground to start a line.

  2. Close the cut

    Reach the border again and you claim the side without the Snarl. No reversing, no crossing your own line, and a line left standing lights a fuse.

  3. Outrun the Snarl

    A storm of living energy that turns toward your open line and lurches without warning. A long cut is never a sure thing.

  4. Chain and charge

    Claims within six seconds chain a combo up to 4×. Close calls and claimed ground fill Overdrive: eight seconds of slowed enemies and double score.

Guardian fights

Fought, not just out-claimed.

Stage ten of every sector is a boss. Every claim drains its health bar, and it fights in a rhythm you can learn.

  1. IdleIt drifts. Your window to cut.
  2. TellIt slows, a ring closes on it, and the attack is named.
  3. AttackVolley, Nova, Charge or Summon.
  4. ExposedIt recovers. Hits land for 1.5×.

Close cuts hit twice as hard

Close your line within reach of a guardian for double damage. A big enough hit staggers it.

Three phases

At two-thirds and one-third health it shifts phase: a longer pattern, shorter tells, less time to breathe.

Living worlds · music

Wherever you draw a line, the light comes back.

A sector’s ten stages are ten chapters of one restoration. The Snarl’s veil thins with every claim; weather thickens, creatures return, lights come back one by one. The world remembers.

  • Layered, painted worlds. Haze, long lights, weather and life drift at their own depths around the board, leaning as you move.
  • A theme for every sector. Every stage has its own song, and a sector’s ten are variations on one theme that builds toward the guardian.
  • Music that answers you. Drawing plucks notes in key, claims land stingers on the beat, and the arena ripples in time.

Accessibility

Built so everyone can read the board.

Eight colour-vision modes, covering red-green, blue-yellow and monochrome vision, remap the colours you must never confuse (border, trail, the Snarl, hazards, the Nexus) and add shape cues that need no colour at all.

  • High contrast dims the backdrop art behind the board.
  • Larger HUD for easier reading at a glance.
  • Reduce motion follows your system setting and stills the living worlds.
  • Reduced flashing takes the flashes out of the arena’s reactions.
  • Screen shake and haptics can be switched off.
  • Game pace: Standard, or Fast at 1.5× for a quicker challenge.
