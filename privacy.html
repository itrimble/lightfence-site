Privacy · Effective October 14, 2026

Lightfence collects no data.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected

Data collection

Lightfence, for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, has no advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts or developer-run server. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, scores, achievements and settings are saved on the device you play on. They are never sent to us.

Controllers

Game controllers and the Siri Remote are read by the game only to play it. Nothing about them leaves your device.

Support email

If you email us, we receive what you choose to include and use it only to reply.

This website

The Lightfence home page may use PostHog for anonymous page analytics. This privacy page and the support page load no analytics. The app itself never includes analytics.

Children

Lightfence collects no information from anyone, including children.
