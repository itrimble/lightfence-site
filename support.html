Help with Lightfence

Questions, bugs or ideas: email iantrimble@remnantsecurity.com with your device and, if you can, the sector and stage. We read every message.

How do I play?

Run the border, steer into open ground to draw a line, and reach the border again to claim the side without the Snarl. Claim the stage’s target to clear it. The first stage teaches you as you go.

Controls

  • iPhone and iPad: drag to steer; tap the Overdrive pill or tap with two fingers for Overdrive.
  • Siri Remote: click the edges of the clickpad ring to steer, click the centre to select, Play/Pause to pause.
  • Game controllers: PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo and MFi pads work on all three. The on-screen hints name your controller’s buttons.

Accessibility

Options has eight colour-vision modes with shape cues, high contrast, a larger HUD, reduced flashing, and switches for screen shake and haptics. Lightfence follows your device’s Reduce Motion setting.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on the device you play on. Progress isn’t synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes it.

Privacy

Lightfence collects no data. See the privacy policy.
